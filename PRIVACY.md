# iTeam Chat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026 · Developer: Devendra Jondhale

**Summary: iTeam Chat has no servers of its own, no ads and no tracking. Your messages and records are end-to-end encrypted.**

## What the app stores
- **On your device:** your chats, tasks, records, files and settings.
- **In iCloud (Apple's CloudKit):** encrypted envelopes that carry messages and files between devices. They cannot be read without your device's keys and are deleted after 45 days. Your public profile (display name, optional photo and an invite code) is stored so contacts can find you.
- **Sign in with Apple:** gives the app your name and email (on Android too, with a free Apple Account). Your email stays on your device unless you choose to share it.
- **Location (optional):** only when you use Nearby (a public listing you choose to publish, rounded to about 1 km), SOS or Check In (sent end-to-end encrypted only to the chats you pick), or a survey location question.
- **Wallet documents** stay only on your device (encrypted on Android) and are never uploaded.
- **Profile details** such as age, sex, mobile and email stay on your device.

## What we do not do
- No advertising, analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No sale or sharing of data with third parties.
- AI features are optional. By default they run on your device with Apple Intelligence. If you add your own OpenAI, Anthropic or Google key, only the text you choose is sent to that provider.
- **Scan & fill:** papers you scan or pick (photos, PDFs) are read on your device. With Apple Intelligence the values are also filled on your device; with your own AI key, only the text read from that paper is sent to your chosen provider. Nothing is saved until you check the values and tap "Use these".
- **Holiday check:** if you allow it, iTeam reads the holidays in your iPhone calendar on the device to warn about hearings or events on holidays. Calendar data never leaves your phone.

## Your choices
- Delete chats or records at any time; deleting the app removes local data.
- **Delete your account:** Settings → Delete my account removes your public profile, Nearby listing and room memberships from iCloud and erases all iTeam data on the device. Without the app, email deven86@me.com from your Apple Account email and we delete your profile within 7 days. Encrypted envelopes already sent expire from iCloud within 45 days.
- Android permissions are asked only when needed: camera (QR codes), microphone (voice notes), location (Nearby, SOS, Check In), notifications (messages and reminders).
- Complaints and module requests sent through the app are not end-to-end encrypted, and the form says so.
