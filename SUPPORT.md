# iTeam Chat — Support

- **In the app:** Settings > Help & complaints.
- **Online:** open an issue at https://github.com/deven86/iTeam/issues
- **Privacy:** see [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).

Common questions
- *Messages stuck "waiting to sync"?* Pull down on Chats to refresh; items for removed contacts are dropped automatically.
- *Where are professional modules?* Modules > Module Store > Get — Free (free for the first year).
