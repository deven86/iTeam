Sign in with Apple is the only login; no phone number or separate account is needed.

To try the app alone, open Chats: demo chats with "Asha" and "Rahul" are created on first launch and reply automatically, so tasks, attendance, finance and other modules can be tested without a second device. Modules > Module Store lists every module; tap "Get — Free" to add one, then "Try a demo" inside it.

Messages and records are end-to-end encrypted on the device (CryptoKit) and passed through the app's CloudKit public database as encrypted envelopes, which are deleted after 45 days. New in 1.0.2: an optional bridge to Android users (Settings > Talk to Android users, off by default) uses the developer's own relay server relay.iteamchat.in, which only stores the same encrypted envelopes until delivered (max 30 days) and is used to send push notifications. Turning it on uses Sign in with Apple; it can be turned off at any time, and Settings > Delete my account removes the relay account too.

User-generated content: Rooms messages have Report, and chat or contact info has "Report and block"; blocked users can no longer message the user, and reports reach the developer through Settings > Help & complaints.

All modules are free during the first year; there are no in-app purchases in this version. AI features are optional (Settings > AI & My Day), use Apple Intelligence on the device by default, and never give medical advice.


New in 1.0.1: Business Autopilot (Settings > AI > Autopilot) is OFF by default. When an owner turns it on, replies are generated from that module's records and always start with "🤖 <name>'s assistant:" so recipients know it is automated; it is limited to 12 replies per chat per day. Scan & fill reads documents on the device. Documents are kept on the device and shared only inside the record's chat.
