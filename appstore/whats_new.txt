Big update — new modules, smarter tools and many fixes.

NEW
• Documents in every module: a proper case file with folders, numbering, index PDF and safe open/share
• Scan & fill: scan a bill, prescription, visiting card or form and iTeam fills the fields for you (Apple Intelligence, on device)
• New modules: Stock & Inventory (QR labels), Admissions, Vendors & Contracts, Procurement, My Home, Lost & Found
• Private grievances to admins and Transfer duties before leaving a group
• My Progress: daily challenges, badges and a productivity score
• Gym: iLife-style daily and weekly reports, morning planner and night review
• Business Autopilot (optional, off by default): an AI assistant that answers client questions from your module records, clearly marked as the assistant

FIXED
• Gym workout no longer freezes or crashes
• File screens no longer close on their own; tapping a file no longer crashes the app
• Demo entries now delete completely
• Clearer help when Apple Health shows no data
